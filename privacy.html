Privacy Policy for OmniPDF

Last Updated: January 6, 2026

Summary: OmniPDF is a "Zero-Data" application. We do not collect, store, or share any of your personal information or document data.

1. Data Collection

OmniPDF is designed to work entirely client-side. When you use any of our PDF or Image tools, all processing occurs locally on your own machine. We do not have servers that store your files, and we do not have access to the contents of the documents you process.

2. Cookies and Tracking

This application does not use cookies, trackers, or any third-party analytics (like Google Analytics). We do not track your location, your device ID, or your usage habits.

3. Third-Party Services

OmniPDF uses the Microsoft Store Digital Goods API for Pro license verification. This process is handled securely by the Microsoft Store; we do not receive or store your payment information (credit cards, bank details, etc.).

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page.
